DeepShield AI: Forensic Edition

2nd Runner-Up, Technovate 2026 (team Cyber Sentinels)

Detects AI-generated images, video and audio, built as a triage tool for cyber-crime cells rather than a plain classifier. Every verdict comes with a traceable explanation, the applicable BNS 2023 and IT Act sections, and a chain-of-custody PDF with file hash and metadata.

  • Image detection ensembles two HuggingFace classifiers (ViT, SigLIP) with Error Level Analysis, FFT and facial symmetry checks in OpenCV.
  • Video adds a frame-to-frame consistency check; audio uses Wav2Vec2 for synthetic voice.
  • Explanations are RAG-grounded and tied to the signals that fired, not free-form LLM text.
  • Perceptual hashing flags recycled evidence across cases; an evaluation script reports accuracy, precision, recall, F1 and latency.

Bhu-Darpan: Land Record Digitization and Validation

Smart India Hackathon 2026, PS 26018. Team leader of six.

Digitizes and validates land records for the Ministry of Rural Development. I built the AI/OCR module: preprocessing, text and field extraction, and confidence scoring, tested on real land record images.

  • Stack: React, Vite, TypeScript, FastAPI, PostgreSQL/PostGIS, background OCR pipeline, review queue and QR-verified certificates.
  • Cadastral mapping with 3D ULPIN is the main differentiator.
  • Qualified the department-level and institute-level rounds.
